The dark

you see I am Jeremy my dad and sister died in a car accident and my mom being the only one who survived gave up on life she stopped taking care of me,going to work and stopped speaking to anyone she was always couped up in her room.


I never really was popular in school I was always bullied because of how i looked and grew up, I was a hardcore geek I liked playing old video games and I loved to draw. Now Im not too good at art but i've been able to draw my dad and sister reaaaaaaaly good!

So one night as im walking home I noticed a black figure and faint white glow,then I went over there it dissapeared but when I was close to home I heard a voice say "jeremy is that you?"

come to reality

I ignored it and went home,plus it probably was just my imagination. But when I got home everything was a mess, then I went to my mom's room and she said this to me "Jeremy its all your fault its all your fault." I was guessing that this was her normal routine you see when she was drunk she would scream and curse, but after you leave her alone for a bit she calms down

This time she didn't she got up and hit me and said this "Why couldn't you have died in the car crash your always getting bullied and im getting laughed at because my son is a weirdo and is only a fraction of his father and your sister is way better than you." I snapped at her "why do you always do this you blame me for something no one could've controlled, Cause mom ever since dad and sister died you been nonstop complaining like shut the hell up you should've died instead of dad and sis."

I packed my things and began running away as I reached the nearby subway I heard the same voice and another voice they both said "come back" and "stay."

Are we dead yet?

* a couple weeks later* It's been three weeks ever since I ran away I just wish everything could go back to normal im just barely surviving as it is I have only one more week worth of food. For some *context* I am friends with a shop keeper who gave me some food but because I went on the train and left town I can't get anything anymore

as I ate my last supply of food and drank the last bottle of water,I finally accepted my fate I would die here.So I had some fun and I went doing whatever I could to pass the time. as I laid to rest I said "I love you dad and sis I hope to come wherever you both are."

I fell to the ground,then I saw a light and woke up in my old classroom.
